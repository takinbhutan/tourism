Must-see Bhutan

Places worth travelling for

Build your route around the places and experiences that excite you. Bhutan's official tourism portal highlights its mix of culture, nature, adventure and slow journeys.

🏔️ Paro & Tiger's Nest

Begin in the Paro Valley and make time for the iconic Taktsang, popularly known as Tiger's Nest. Bhutan Tourism describes it as one of the country's must-do experiences.

Culture • Hike • Views

🏙️ Thimphu

Explore Bhutan's capital through its dzongs, markets, cultural sights and mountain setting. Buddha Dordenma overlooks the Thimphu Valley.

Culture • City • Food

🏯 Punakha

Discover Punakha Dzong, the valley landscape and the famous suspension bridge over the river.

Heritage • Valley • River

🏔️ Dochula Pass

A high mountain pass with 108 memorial chortens and Himalayan views when weather allows.

Mountains • Viewpoint

🌿 Gangtey Valley

A peaceful valley known for its natural setting and seasonal black-necked crane experiences from November to mid-February.

Nature • Birding • Slow travel

🌄 Bumthang

For travellers who want to go deeper into Bhutan, Bumthang opens the door to quieter valleys, temples and longer journeys beyond the classic west.

Offbeat • Spiritual • Nature
The Bhutan feeling

Not just sightseeing.

Bhutan's appeal is the combination of landscape, living traditions and a slower rhythm. The official tourism portal describes the country through nature, heritage, culture, adventure and wellbeing.

  • Try traditional Bhutanese food.
  • Experience archery, the national sport.
  • Spend time in monasteries and dzongs respectfully.
  • Leave room in the itinerary for mountain roads and viewpoints.
