Questions

Frequently asked.

Can you plan a completely custom Bhutan trip?

Yes. Tell us your travel dates, number of travellers, preferred destinations, hotel category and approximate budget.

Do you arrange vehicles?

Vehicle options can be included depending on destination, group size, route and availability.

Can I book only a hotel or vehicle?

You can mention exactly what you need in the enquiry form. We'll let you know what options are available.

Do you cover Dooars offbeat destinations?

Yes. Samsing, Suntaleykhola, Rocky Island, Jhalong, Bindu, Murti, Raimatang, Jayanti, Buxa and other North Bengal destinations can be included in custom plans.

Will every booking be given to another travel agent?

No. Our aim is to handle trips directly whenever possible. Partner coordination is intended only for selected special requirements or cases where additional local support is useful.

Are the prices fixed on the website?

No. We prefer current quotations because prices can change according to season, hotel, vehicle, group size, availability and travel requirements.
